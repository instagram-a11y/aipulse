9. Functional & Technical Requirements for Claude Code
Recommended stack: Next.js 15+ App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui primitives where appropriate, Framer Motion for restrained interactions.
Internationalization: /en and /fa routes; locale switch preserves the current page; Persian uses dir="rtl" and English dir="ltr" at document level.
CMS: Sanity, Payload or Strapi for services, industries, case studies, articles, team and FAQs. Choose one and document the schema.
Forms: server-side validation, spam protection, consent checkbox, CRM/webhook integration, email notification and success state.
Booking: Calendly/Cal.com integration or a custom calendar handoff; display times in the visitor’s time zone.
Analytics: GA4 + Search Console + event tracking for CTA, audit start/complete, form submit, WhatsApp, language and case-study views.
SEO: localized metadata, canonical/hreflang, Open Graph, sitemap, robots, Organization/Service/FAQ schema and clean slugs.
Performance: optimized responsive images, next/font, lazy loading, minimal client JS, Core Web Vitals target “Good”.
Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, labels, error announcements and reduced motion.
Security/privacy: collect minimum data, keep secrets server-side, sanitize inputs, rate-limit endpoints, define retention and do not send confidential client data to AI models without explicit controls.
Architecture: reusable bilingual content objects/components; no English strings hard-coded into shared UI components.

13. Strategic Recommendations | پیشنهادهای نهایی
Lead with business outcomes, not model names. Clients buy saved time, higher conversion and better service—not “GPT” itself.
Choose 4–6 priority industries for the first launch. Keep the full industry library, but do not dilute the homepage.
Use an “AI Opportunity Audit” as the main lead magnet; it demonstrates the method before the sales call.
Publish small interactive demos: lead-response simulator, adaptive-learning sample and appointment assistant. Demos convert better than abstract claims.
Create separate landing pages for Iran and international campaigns when offers, currency, contact channels or compliance differ.
Do not promise replacing people. Position Pulse AI as removing low-value work, increasing capacity and keeping human approval where judgment matters.
Every case study should show baseline, implementation, human controls and measurable outcome. Trust will be the strongest differentiator.
Final brand idea: AIPulse gives every business an intelligent operating pulse—connected, responsive and continuously improving.
ایده نهایی برند: AIPulse به هر کسب‌وکار یک نبض عملیاتی هوشمند می‌دهد؛ متصل، پاسخ‌گو و در حال بهبود مستمر.
